  • Document behaviour and coordinate with functional/product stakeholders

How the market reads against your profile

This week's demand is unambiguous: employers want a junior-to-mid engineer who can stand up REST APIs over a relational database and keep them fast, with the language split roughly evenly between Java/Spring Boot and C#/.NET, and React expected as the front-end half. Databases (7/10) and SQL (6/10) are the only near-universal requirements; cloud is asked for mostly as Azure (3/10), not AWS (2/10). Manik matches the Java/Spring Boot, Kafka, PostgreSQL and React core strongly, and his throughput and export-time numbers are exactly the 'high-load systems' evidence JD8 and JD2 are screening for. The real exposure is threefold: Azure and SQL Server/EF Core (the whole .NET half of this market, JD1/JD3/JD8), the vocabulary gaps the matcher flagged — Agile/Scrum, concurrency, profiling, NoSQL — and experience framing, since two years against JD3's stated 3-5 years means he must lead with measured impact rather than tenure.
